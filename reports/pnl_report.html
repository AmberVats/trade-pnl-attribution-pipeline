PnL Calculation Methodology

Realized PnL = Sold Quantity × (Sell Price − Average Cost)

Unrealized PnL = Closing Quantity × (Market Price − Average Cost)

Total PnL = Realized PnL + Unrealized PnL

PnL Attribution separates trading-related PnL from price-related PnL.
